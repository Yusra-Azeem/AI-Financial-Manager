# Bank Lending Policy Handbook

This document describes the bank's lending policy across all loan products.
It is the source of truth used by the AI assistant when explaining why a
customer received a specific offer, rate, or amount limit.

## 1. Education Loans

Education loans are available to salaried and self-employed applicants with a
minimum credit score of 650. The maximum loan amount is capped at 3 times the
applicant's verified annual income. The base interest rate is 8.5% per annum,
with a discount of 0.5% for credit scores of 750 and above, and a further
0.5% discount for scores of 800 and above. The maximum repayment tenure is 84
months (7 years). Applicants must provide proof of admission or enrollment
for the loan to be disbursed. Interest-only payments are permitted during the
study period at the customer's request, with full EMI resuming after course
completion.

## 2. Business Loans

Business loans require a minimum credit score of 700, reflecting the higher
risk profile of new or growing businesses. The maximum loan amount is capped
at 4 times verified annual income. The base interest rate is 11.0% per annum,
with the same credit-score-based discounts as education loans (0.5% at 750+,
an additional 0.5% at 800+). Maximum tenure is 60 months (5 years). The bank
requires business registration documents and, for loans above the standard
cap, a basic business plan or cash-flow projection. Business loans are
reviewed more frequently than other loan types due to higher variability in
repayment ability.

## 3. Home Loans

Home loans are our most conservative product from a rate perspective, given
the collateral involved. Minimum credit score required is 700. Maximum loan
amount is capped at 6 times verified annual income, the highest multiple we
offer, reflecting the long repayment horizon. Base interest rate is 8.0% per
annum, with standard credit-score discounts applying. Maximum tenure is 240
months (20 years). Property valuation and title verification are mandatory
before disbursement.

## 4. Vehicle Loans

Vehicle loans require a minimum credit score of 650. Maximum loan amount is
capped at 2 times verified annual income. Base interest rate is 9.5% per
annum, with standard credit-score discounts applying. Maximum tenure is 84
months (7 years). The vehicle itself serves as collateral, and loan-to-value
ratio may not exceed 90% of the vehicle's on-road price.

## 5. Medical Loans

Medical loans have the lowest eligibility bar, given the urgent and often
unplanned nature of medical expenses. Minimum credit score is 600. Maximum
loan amount is capped at 1.5 times verified annual income — intentionally
conservative because these loans are typically unsecured and disbursed
quickly, sometimes within 24 hours. Base interest rate is 10.0% per annum,
with standard credit-score discounts applying. Maximum tenure is 48 months (4
years). Medical loans can be approved with minimal documentation, generally
just a hospital estimate or invoice.

## 6. Interest Rate Discount Policy

All loan types follow the same credit-score discount structure: applicants
with a credit score of 750–799 receive a 0.5% discount off the base rate.
Applicants with a credit score of 800 or above receive an additional 0.5%
discount (1.0% total off base). Applicants below 700 (or the product-specific
minimum, whichever is higher) receive no discount and may be charged a 1.0%
premium above the base rate to offset elevated risk.

## 7. Loan Amount Capping

If a customer's requested loan amount exceeds the maximum allowed for their
loan type and income level, the bank does not reject the application. Instead,
the requested amount is automatically capped at the maximum eligible amount,
and the customer is informed of this cap along with the reasoning (income
multiple exceeded).

## 8. Negotiation Policy

When a customer proposes an interest rate different from the bank's standard
policy rate, the system allows up to 3 rounds of negotiation. If the
customer's proposed rate is within 0.25% of the policy rate, the bank accepts
immediately. Otherwise, the bank concedes gradually toward the customer's ask
each round, weighted 70% toward the policy rate, to protect margins while
remaining flexible. If no agreement is reached after 3 rounds, the bank holds
firm at its original policy rate as the final offer.

## 9. Savings and Spending Monitoring

Independently of loan products, the bank monitors customer spending against
their stated monthly savings goal. If a customer's actual savings fall below
their goal, this is flagged as a shortfall and may prompt a proactive
spending warning notification, separate from any loan offer.
